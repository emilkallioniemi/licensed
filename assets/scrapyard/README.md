# Static scrapyard kit

Original agent-authored Blender assets for ticket 08a. No third-party models, textures, fonts, or audio. Palette and rounded industrial forms follow the modeled truck and waiting room. `source/build_scrapyard.py` rebuilds all nine GLBs and `source/scrapyard.blend` using Blender 5.2.1. Run Blender with `--background --python` and the absolute generator path. Source objects remain editable inside each named module; the generator retains individual components before joining for export. The blend is a module library at shared origins, not a laid-out yard.

`wreck`, `wreck_stack`, `scrap_pile`, `container`, `fence`, `workshop`, `industrial_skyline`, `parking_wreck`, and `gate_post` use metres and ground-centred pivots (Blender Z up, GLB/Godot Y up). Each module exports one mesh with a small shared material palette. Wrecks include dusty glazing, crushed roof/bodywork, tyres/rims, bumpers, lamps and paint loss; containers have corrugations, locks and hinges. The workshop has closed shutters, clerestory windows, roof/vent/drain details. Everything is static.

`Scrapyard` places these modules in the actual TestArea. Reachable props use static triangle collision on world/truck-contact layer 5, while ground/bump layer 9 remains unchanged. The four gate posts and two parking wrecks dress the original box collision footprints. Bump top seams and flush repairs/drains are visual only. Surface aggregate is one 1,600-instance MultiMesh; the continuous ground shader adds dust, fine aggregate, tar seams and old tyre wear without new surface physics. Distant workshop/silos and dense salvage stock are beyond the visible perimeter fence and also have solid collision. Four lower ground strips outside the unchanged apron support learners who climb the fence. No route exits, moving machines, new controls, scoring or hazards are introduced.

Clear apron margins preserve current stairs, gates, reversing, parking, side-bank/uneven-ground samples and under-truck rescue. Future route work must replace the perimeter/placement deliberately. This kit does not implement the five route sections, ravine, bridge, crusher, workers, destruction, audio, or human Steam acceptance. Runtime evidence is in `.scratch/monster-truck-build/08a-evidence.md`.
