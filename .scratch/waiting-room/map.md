# Map: the waiting room (pre-game)

Label: wayfinder:map
Charted: 2026-09-12

## Destination

A spec at `.scratch/waiting-room/spec.md` for everything before the driving test starts: three friends launch a GitHub-downloaded Windows build with Steam running, spawn straight into a walkable 3D test-centre waiting room (no main menu), invite each other from the reception desk, book the vehicle whose test they want to sit and pick the role they want to play, ready up, and land together in a stub scene that proves role and input travel across the network. Tickets get cut from that spec afterwards; this map decides, it does not build.

## Notes

- Networking: Steam only, via the GodotSteam GDExtension and its `SteamMultiplayerPeer` plugged into Godot's high-level multiplayer. See `docs/adr/0002-steam-only-networking.md`. Exactly three players is `docs/adr/0001-exactly-three-players.md`.
- Design judgment: read `docs/design.md` before any ticket that touches a screen, a station, or an examiner line; log steering in `docs/corrections.md`. Watch the *Polish before funny* and *Scaling* drifts in particular: the kit below is already art, and the solo-testing seam must stay a dev seam, not a mode.
- **Asset kit (commit `ae12dc5`)**: use it, do not greybox. `assets/waiting_room/waiting_room.tscn` is the room (12 × 10 m, entrance at Z=+5, reception and booking board toward Z=-5, three numbered chairs, a closed "Test Area" door, a spare notice board) with `AttachmentPoints` markers for `Entrance`, `ReceptionApproach`, `FriendsScreen`, `BookingBoardApproach`, `FutureDisplay`. The booking board already has the five slots (`MonsterTruckSlot`, `CarSlot`, `MopedSlot`, `TruckTrailerSlot`, `HelicopterSlot`, each with a `DisplaySurface`) and modelled padlocks. The friends terminal has a `FriendsScreenSurface` sized for a 740 × 460 SubViewport. `assets/slice_0/player/learner.tscn` is the learner: rigid meshes, no rig, six exported colours. `assets/slice_0/music/please_take_a_number.wav` is the waiting-room loop. Both READMEs in `assets/` say what is and is not included; the kit has no scripts, collisions, controller, or interaction.

- Platform: Windows only. Distribution: a GitHub release zip of the export (`exe + pck + libgodotsteam DLL + steam_api64.dll`). No `steam_appid.txt`: passing 480 to `steamInitEx` covers it.
- Skills: `grilling` + `domain-modeling` for grilling tickets; `prototype` for prototype tickets; `research` for research tickets. Glossary in `CONTEXT.md`; use its terms in ticket titles and the spec.

### Fixed by the grilling that charted this map

These were decided in conversation before any ticket existed, so they live here rather than in Decisions so far.

- No main menu. The build opens directly into the waiting room as a single-player-so-far session; the room *is* the lobby.
- Inviting happens in-world at the reception desk, with an in-game friends list. No Steam overlay required. Joiners appear through the entrance.
- Each player chooses the vehicle whose test they want to sit; disagreement is visible and must be resolved by the players before it becomes the booking. Nobody's choice overrides anyone's.
- Roles are first-come exclusive; a taken role is unavailable to others. A "random" pick fills whatever is left at launch.
- The monster truck's roles are **Driver**, **Spotter**, **Navigator**.
- The booking board shows the monster truck (selectable) and four locked roadmap vehicles: car, moped, truck and trailer, helicopter, each with a padlock and its one-line joke from the README.
- Players are their Steam name and avatar. No learner customization *by the player* in this slice; the kit's six learner colours may be dealt per player so the three are tellable apart (that is the ticket's call, not a customization system).
- Exactly three players to launch, with a debug override for testing alone.
- The launch target is a stub: the three learners standing together somewhere, able to run around, with their roles visible. Nothing to do there yet.
- In-game voice is wanted ("makes it a game instantly") if it is not too hard; see the voice tickets.

- [The reception desk: inviting and arriving](issues/06-reception-desk.md): decided in conversation (retyped to grilling). Every waiting room is a FRIENDS_ONLY **room** from boot, so friends can **Join** without an invite. Desk screen: "Reception, 1 of 3" header; rows of avatar, name, state, in groups *In the waiting room* / *At the test centre* (Invite + Join) / *Online. Not at the test centre.* (no verbs); offline hidden; signage register, not the examiner's. Invites arrive in-world at the desk (row with Accept/Ignore, changed station prompt, a placeholder ring); no expiry, staleness checked on Accept with three dry failure lines. Only a lone learner joins or accepts ("You have company." otherwise); a guest can **Leave** back to a room of their own from the desk (Emil's steer), the host cannot. Arrival: joiner fades out and up in the doorway; everyone else sees the entrance door open and close with a placeholder sound; no examiner line. Host-owned state, nothing resets on join, leaving frees pick and role. Dev transport: real list, verbs disabled, footer "Dev transport."
- [Role pickup: Driver, Spotter, Navigator, or random](issues/08-role-pickup.md): decided in conversation (retyped to grilling; Emil gave free hands). Click a free button to hold, click again to drop, click another to swap; a taken button does nothing; same-frame ties go to host order, no error. Held strip floods with the holder's palette colour and Steam name, lamp lit to match; free is blank as the kit ships. Name tag gets a second line with the held role (the same line the stub shows). Random is **not exclusive**: palette pips on its button, dealt shuffled by the host at launch confirmation, theatre handed to Ready-up. Roles are **gated on the booking**: dead until one exists (as The booking board defines it), every role released when the booking dissolves, strips read "No booking." until then. Same rules under the dev transport.
- [The booking board: agreeing on a vehicle](issues/07-booking-board.md): decided in conversation (retyped to grilling; Emil gave free hands). A **booking** forms when all three players in the room have picked the same vehicle, and dissolves when a pick changes or a player leaves; nobody can join at three, so arrivals never dissolve it. Rows are drawn in two lines on the kit's `DisplaySurface` (label meshes hidden, padlocks kept): vehicle name on top, then either the **pick chip** line (palette flood + Steam name, arrival order) or, on locked rows, one signage-register line ("Seats one. Party of three."). Pick, drop, switch by clicking; no pre-pick; clicking a locked row does nothing; disagreement is quiet. Booking: the row reads BOOKED, the role column wakes, one placeholder sound; dissolution is silent. Amends Testing alone: `--min-players` gates the booking too. Unblocks Ready-up.
- [Ready-up and the launch stub](issues/09-ready-up-and-launch.md): decided in conversation (Emil gave free hands). **Sitting is readying**: any chair, E to sit, E or a move key to stand, the chair floods with the sitter's palette, the chair never refuses. The kit's spare **notice board** becomes the room's one sign, stating in signage register what is missing ("Waiting for 2." / "No booking." / "Roles: 2 of 3." / "Seated: 1 of 3.") and counting "Monster truck. 3." to 1 once nothing is; any stand, pick, role or departure cancels silently. The examiner's only line in the room is the call, **"Monster truck."**, TTS, no body. Fade to black, door sound, music fades on the player; no door animation. Random dealt at the end of the count, revealed in the **test area**: others' roles on the name tag, your own as a corner line. The stub is a car park: three bays, a MONSTER TRUCK sign, voice on, nothing to do. Host-only "Back to the waiting room" in the Escape overlay returns everyone through the entrance with all state cleared; a drop in the test area sends the rest back too. One kit request: a seated learner.
- [Write the spec](issues/11-write-the-spec.md): **the map's arrival.** [`spec.md`](spec.md) exists, `ready-for-agent`, collapsing tickets 01 to 10 in the order a player meets them, with all copy in one table, the two kit requests in one place, the Not yet specified lines below as its build-time list, and one testing seam (the host-owned room state, headless). ## Not yet specified

- **Returning to the waiting room after a test.** Ready-up decided the mechanism for the stub (host-only Back, everyone arrives through the entrance, all state cleared). Whether a *finished test* returns the same way, whether the room doubles as the results screen, and how the license card shows up, is Slice 2 territory; the room's layout should leave space for it.
- **Departure theatre in the room.** The reception desk decided the *mechanism* (a guest who leaves, or whose host vanishes, lands in a fresh room of their own; leaving frees pick and role and dissolves the booking) and Ready-up decided what happens on a drop in the test area (the rest go back to the room). Still open: what the room *shows* when a friend drops mid-room; today the learner vanishes with no door and no sound. A build-time call for the spec's open list, not a ticket.
- **Shoving.** Learners are solid and do not push each other in this slice. If standing still in front of a friend is not funny enough in the first build, shoving (the pushed client moves itself on overlap) is the feel item to add. Sharpens once three people have stood at the board.
- **What the test area grows into.** It is Slice 1's starting scene (the truck cab, three cameras). Out of this map's scope to design; the stub commits Slice 1 to exactly two elements, the corner role line and the booked-vehicle sign, and nothing else.
