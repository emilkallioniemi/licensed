# What does a complete monster truck test look like?

Label: wayfinder:grilling
Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Question

Should the vehicle contain one full test or several smaller tests, and how do driving and manoeuvres connect within an attempt? Determine candidate format, successful-attempt duration, and progression toward the vehicle's license. A route, course, isolated manoeuvres, and a mix remain alternatives; 10–15 minutes was only an agent suggestion. Identify what must be compared through play before committing, and reconcile the vehicle/test vocabulary when a decision is made.

## Answer

Resolved with the user on 2026-09-12 as a candidate structure requiring playable validation.

- One continuous test combines driving and several manoeuvres, with one shared pass/fail result. Passing earns the monster-truck stamp; there are no separately passed smaller tests. This retains the existing Vehicle, Test, and Stamp glossary relationships.
- The setting is a mountainside scrapyard built over a ravine: stacked wrecks, scrap piles, narrow bridges, and a working crusher provide the setting's direction. Ordinary driving-test demands in an absurd, dangerous location create the comedy. The test area need not be geographically adjacent to the waiting room.
- The scrapyard remains active during the test, including workers and moving machinery. Exact hazards and behaviour are not settled; forklifts and suspended cars were examples, not a locked roster.
- The examiner directs a route and gives manoeuvre instructions at particular places. Driving between manoeuvres is scored and exposes players to hazards and consequences from earlier mistakes. Parking finishes the route.
- Begin with roughly five minutes for a successful attempt. This is a pacing target for playtesting, not the timer limit. Serious-fault termination and scoring remain in their own decision.
- Validate that every player has enough action, connecting driving stays entertaining, and moving hazards create avoidable chaos. Revise route, pacing, and hazards first if those checks fail; conversation does not prove the format works.
