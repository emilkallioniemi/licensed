# How do players recover after leaving or overturning the truck?

Label: wayfinder:grilling
Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Parent: ../map.md
Blocked by: 01, 03

## Question

Given free movement at any time, including while the truck moves, what happens when a player jumps or falls off, is left behind, is trapped, or the truck overturns? Decide how players get back aboard or recover, what remains playable during the aftermath, and how recovery relates to the test's result and termination policy. Preserve shared physical comedy and avoid an indefinite state where a required player cannot participate.

Distinguish ordinary climbing and boarding from any exceptional recovery action. Determine who can trigger recovery, what it changes, and what needs playable evidence. Do not quietly turn recovery into an extra vehicle mode or decide serious-fault termination again here.

## Answer

Resolved with the user on 2026-09-12. Recovery follows physical problem solving, with the timer continuing throughout.

- Start with secure footing during ordinary driving. Sharp turns, big bumps, collisions, and walking over the edge can throw a learner off. This handling candidate needs three-human playtesting; falling should create occasional shared problems without making roof riding constantly frustrating.
- A harmless fall is recoverable and does not itself cause a fault. The learner walks back and physically climbs aboard; the other players can stop or reverse to collect them. Players remain free to leave controls and move around as previously agreed.
- A trapped learner must be freed physically, for example by another player taking over an available control and moving the truck. There is no teleport or get-unstuck action. Being trapped is distinct from being catastrophically crushed.
- If physical rescue proves impossible, the group may concede the failed attempt and retry instead of waiting for the timer to expire. Conceding abandons the attempt; it cannot rescue a learner within the same test. [How are results, best ratings, and retries shared?](11-results-and-retries.md) owns who initiates concession, what agreement is required, and its presentation and transition into retry.
- Overturning onto the truck's side is a serious fault, with no truck-righting action to resume the test. Catastrophic learner accidents, including falling into the ravine or being crushed, also guarantee shared failure even when the truck remains usable. Let the physical aftermath and examiner response play out before showing the result, following the existing failure decision. Recovery cannot undo guaranteed failure.

During the actual implementation checkpoints, test ordinary roof riding, a recoverable fall and collection, freeing a trapped control operator, and an impossible rescue followed by concession. Use exactly three humans to judge whether falls are readable and funny, whether physical rescue produces enjoyable cooperation under time pressure, and whether ordinary boarding is reliable. Tune footing, ejection sensitivity, boarding geometry, and rescue space if falls become constant or rescue is routinely futile; do not substitute teleport recovery. Exercise overturning and catastrophic learner accidents to verify shared failure and aftermath timing. These are future validation requirements, not gameplay evidence gathered in this planning session.
